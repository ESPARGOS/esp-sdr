ESP32 family DC retuning and background calibration recovery — 2026-10-08

Implemented as separate changes, crediting 14sea's PR #9 investigation:
https://github.com/ESPARGOS/esp-sdr/pull/9
See pr9-review.txt for the original four-chip comparison and PR analysis.

Implementation

Retunes preserve startup RX IQ correction and redo DC calibration at the
requested LO. They no longer deliberately rerun the radiating IQ sweep.
H2 needs four startup IQ pairs, one per RF gain group: measure these once at
boot and reuse them when rebuilding its gain table. A single factory IQ pair
was tested and rejected because it produced much larger DC offsets.
The ESP32 manual-gain release register address is also corrected.

A second change detects actual PHY DC measurements and remeasures DC at the
requested LO before the next capture, even when the requested frequency did
not change. It covers all ten families and both S31 profiles. Eight families
wrap the pinned PHY archive's measurement function and count completions.
ESP32/S2 internal Xtensa calls bypass linker wrapping, so those families
compare the exported DC tables instead. Table sizes and private function
ABIs depend on the pinned SDK versions and must be requalified on upgrades.

The PHY mutex serializes DC recalibration and table snapshots with tracking.
It is not held throughout acquisition: tracking can affect a capture already
in progress; the next preparation repairs calibration. S31 streaming stops
DMA and uses its normal reconfiguration/stream-epoch path before resuming.
H2 and S31's existing disabled periodic PLL tracking remains disabled.
Startup and autonomous PHY calibration can still transmit. This is not a
claim that the whole firmware is receive-only or emits no RF energy.

Bench and coverage

Nine distinct USB-accessible boards: C5, C6, S3, C3, H2, C61, S2, S31 and C2
(ESP8684H). Original ESP32 was not connected. H2's FT232 connection shares the
same board as native USB; UART capture was also checked, but it is not a tenth
board. S2 has no antenna. S31 was accessible by UART, not a usable native USB
or Ethernet streaming connection.

HackRF One and nearby antennas, no coax/attenuators or screened enclosure.
Retuning alternated 2350/2351 MHz with 40 commands (39 actual changes), using
8 MS/s HackRF captures centred at LO+4 MHz to inspect the LO+5 MHz IQ tone.

H2's baseline tone was not detected even in an additional 20 MHz-wide search
around LO. Its zero count therefore does not establish an RF improvement or
exclude emissions elsewhere. The seven positive-baseline results support
removing the repeated IQ sweep, not broadening it with more register guards.

Recovery trials used an instrumented build to invoke the real DC measurement
at 2412 MHz without updating the receiver's requested frequency or saved
calibration state. At each of 2350, 2370, 2413 and 2484 MHz, five repetitions
checked fresh -> stale -> fresh, a CRC-valid capture without another FREQ
command, and retention of manual gain. All 180 trials passed. S2 explicitly
verified table-snapshot detection after its measurement wrapper failed to
observe internal calls. These are fault-injection tests, not controlled
physical temperature cycling. The original C5 tracker experiment is in the
historical report; its temperature stamp missed 10 of 20 forced actual
recalibrations, which motivates observing DC work instead of that stamp.

With gain capped at 45, maximum post-recovery DC magnitudes in ADC counts:
C2 6.8; C3 8.0; C5 12.4; C6 6.5; C61 12.2; H2 43.2; S2 12.6;
S3 9.5; S31 19.6. Earlier maximum-gain stress captures can saturate and are
retained separately; this is not a guarantee of small offsets at every gain.

All eight antenna-equipped boards received HackRF tones at 2352 and 2372 MHz
with LO at 2350 and 2370 MHz. Each used gain settling/adaptation followed by
four recorded captures per LO. Minimum measured tone SNR exceeded 35 dB.
H2 image rejection varied: the cached-IQ candidate measured >=22.9 dB versus
>=23.0 dB baseline, while the final explicit-boot version measured >=17.8 dB.
These nearby-antenna runs establish reception, not equivalent image rejection
at all temperatures/frequencies or across boot calibrations. Keeping boot IQ
coefficients trades repeated radiating calibration for possible IQ drift.

H2 also retains a pre-existing gain-dependent DC bias on some large retunes
with manual gain held at 45: repeated 2350/2484 MHz jumps reached median DC
magnitudes about 250 baseline and 236 cached-IQ. This patch does not solve
that separate issue. Ordinary FREQ-then-GAIN and recovery tests were cleaner.

C2's CH340 occasionally lost bytes on long transfers at 2 Mbaud and even
1 Mbaud; CRC detects this. Recovery used 1024 samples at 1 Mbaud and passed
20/20. Others used 4096 samples. H2 was sampled at 32 MS/s, the others at
80 MS/s. S31 UART tests also used 1 Mbaud. S2 has functional/CRC/calibration
coverage only. S31 streaming has build and host coverage, not hardware
streaming validation. Original ESP32 has build and host coverage only.

Validation and reproducibility

The standalone retune commit passed 51 host tests; combined code passed 53.
All 11 profiles were compiled against their repository-pinned SDKs:
common 25fe69f946311abdaf9ad56591f25fedbc20ac98;
S31 burst 26dd2948925d44f41e3bb614fb044b7a0839a242;
S31 stream c712a0dde385d659a1470a136251980d31a70bc1.
Final production build/flash results are recorded separately in the local
artifacts/dc-retune-all/final-verification.json, including installed revision.

Local ignored evidence: artifacts/pr9-review/ and artifacts/dc-retune-all/.
The latter holds baseline/fixed IQ captures and analyses, per-board recovery
JSON, tone JSON/NPZ, H2 variant comparisons, host/build/flash logs, exported
firmware, and flash backups. scripts/ contains the test harnesses. Diagnostic
commands are not part of production firmware. Experimental version strings
can refer to dirty intermediate commits; retain the matching binaries and
logs rather than identifying those experiments solely by their version text.

These tests do not establish regulatory compliance, calibrated sensitivity,
full tuning-range performance, 5 GHz performance, or long-term thermal
stability. No changes were made to the user's /opt/esp32/esp-idf checkout.
